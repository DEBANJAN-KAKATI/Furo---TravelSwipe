FAQ

Good questions

Is Furo free?

Yes. There's no account and no paywall. Your trips are stored on your own device.

Where does the place information come from?

Live city decks are built from Wikivoyage traveller listings, Wikipedia articles and OpenStreetMap data, and weather comes from Open-Meteo. Bengaluru, Guwahati and Pondicherry also have hand-curated decks. Photos are real pictures of each place from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons — if none exist yet, Furo shows a placeholder rather than a stock photo.

Does it work offline?

Once a city is built it's saved on your device, so you can browse your deck and trip without signal. Maps tiles, weather and building new cities need a connection.

Is there a phone app?

Yes — download the Android APK above, or open Furo in your phone's browser and use “Add to home screen”.

Can I plan with friends?

You can share your full plan as text with route links. Group swiping is in preview — your squad's votes are simulated on your device for now.

How accurate are opening hours and prices?

They come from community-maintained sources. They're usually right, but check on the day — especially around holidays and festivals.
